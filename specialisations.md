# Loot Specialisations

## Amulets

#### Empowered Identity
The base affixes of amulets you find are always upgraded once by default.

#### Meet Cute
Amulets you get from loot rolls always start at tier 2.

#### Open Heart
Your amulets may have up to 6 affixes on them. When you first acquire this 6th affix, you choose it from your amulet's upgrade pool. Additionally, when upgrading affixes in the future, this 6th affix is always available as an additional upgrade option.

#### Reputation Quest
Whenever you would add a new affix to an amulet, you may choose to not add it after seeing what it would be, instead putting a random mission into that affix slot. The next time you complete this mission, a new random affix from the amulet's pool is added to replace it, which is upgraded twice and cannot be the affix you originally chose not to add. If you would upgrade your amulet and an incomplete mission would be chosen as the upgrade candidate, the resulting affix will be upgraded an additional time, and you may choose to replace the mission with a new one at the same time.

#### Soul Refinement
Whenever you upgrade an amulet, you can choose between two different upgrade options.

#### Union of Souls
Whenever you find an amulet, you receive 3 options for which amulet to find. Additionally, whenever you upgrade your amulet with another one from the same set, an existing affix is upgraded twice, or a new affix may be chosen to come from either amulet's upgrade pool (you see both options and choose one).

## Crystals

#### Active Lapidary
Whenever a power is added to a crystal (including when you find one), you can choose between 2 powers from its pool. You can also immediately choose from an additional option for each power your current crystal already has.

#### Extra Socket
You can equip an additional crystal, but it cannot flare.

#### Passive Lapidary
Whenever you find a crystal containing a monster with multiple passive options, you can choose between all of its passive options. You can also do this immediately with your current crystal.

#### Resonance
Your crystal damage base die size increases to a d8.

#### Unstable Power
Your crystal flare chance is increased by 15%.

#### Versatile Power
Your crystals also gain a new random power at rank 3.

## Dream Mirrors

#### Aspiration
Whenever you gain a new affix, you can choose which inspiration it comes from.

#### Carpe Diem
Whenever you give an upgrade point to an affix on you which simply makes progress towards an actual improvement, the affix gains 2 upgrade points instead.

#### Fork in the Road
Whenever you gain a new affix or upgrade/replace an existing affix on you, you gain 1 additional option to choose from.

#### Forward Thinking
Whenever you choose a new inspiration for your dream pool, you can also choose an Affinity for each of the affixes to have (you can choose an Affinity more than once).

#### Genetics
New affixes added to you have 1 higher minimum point value. Additionally, 1 upgrade point is immediately granted to each affix already on you.

#### The Grind
Every 3 times you upgrade a specific affix on you, it gains 4 additional upgrade points.

## Glyphs

#### Controlled Mutation
Every time you gain the 5th or 10th tier of a glyph path, 3 Mutations are revealed to you, and you choose one to gain.

#### Corruption Storm
You gain a new glyphic power that you can use once per combat as a glyphic action for each glyph path you have embarked on. Each power is unique to that path. Additionally, you gain glyphic harm equal to your level.

#### Cross-Contamination
Whenever you choose a new glyph path, you may also choose one of the path options you didn't choose. 3 random tiers from this other path are shown to you, and you may choose one of them to immediately gain. Additionally, for each glyph path you have already started, you immediately do this with two random other paths.

#### Extreme Corruption
All glyph paths have a powerful 11th tier. If this causes you to have multiple unfinished paths, you choose which one to progress when using glyphs.

#### Rapid Corruption
Whenever you start a new glyph path, you gain the first three tiers. Additionally, you immediately gain 2 glyphs for each path you have already started.

#### Rejection and Adoption
Whenever you consume a glyph, roll 2d6, declaring one as positive and the other as negative. You then Reject, gaining the below bonus/penalty based on the roll results. These effects persist on you until you gain an effect that conflicts (polarity-wise) with an effect you already have on you. When this happens, you Adopt, losing all of these Rejection effects and permanently gaining a random enchanted item affix with a minimum point value equal to the number of times you Rejected since you last Adopted.
1. +/- 1 AB
2. +/- 5 max HP
3. +/- 3 damage
4. +/- 1 to all defences
5. +/- 1 to all skill checks
6. your choice of another result

## Relics

#### Generic Evolution
Whenever a new thematic affix would be added to a relic, you are also shown a random enchanted item affix. You may add either to the relic. Additionally, you are immediately shown a random enchanted item affix for each non-starting affix on any relics you currently have, which will have the same amount of upgrade points applied to it as the relic's current affix. You may choose to replace any of the relic's current affixes.

#### Divergence
Whenever you level up a relic, you can choose between two different upgrade options.

#### Dominant Gene
Your relics may have up to 7 affixes on them. When you first acquire this 7th affix, you choose it from the relic's thematic pool. Additionally, when upgrading affixes in the future, this 7th affix is always available as an additional upgrade option.

#### Potent Ancestry
The starting affixes on relics you find have a minimum point value of 2. Additionally, this immediately upgrades any starting affixes on relics you are currently wearing if they started with 1 point.

#### Potent Descendents
The thematic affixes of your relics have a minimum point value of 2. Additionally, this immediately upgrades any thematic affixes on relics you currently have if they started with 1 point.

#### Min-Max
After seeing a new relic you find, you may choose for its negative starting affixes to have double potency, and the resulting extra points are distributed randomly across the rest of the starting affixes. Additionally, you may choose to immediately double the original potency of any negative starting affixes on relics you are currently wearing and distribute the resulting extra points randomly across the other starting affixes on those relics.

If a relic you find has no negative affixes on it and you wish to use this effect, you may add a random enchanted item affix to it with a maximum point value of -4, then add the resulting extra points randomly to the other starting affixes.

## Shrines

#### Conversion
Whenever you find a shrine, you may choose to reroll it into a different shrine type.

#### Desperate Prayer
After you use a shrine, you may apply a random other shrine to the same item. This extra shrine must be used once revealed.

#### Piety
Any affixes added or positively modified by your shrine crafts have +1 minimum point value, after the craft.

#### Reject Pact
After you use a shrine, you may undo the effects of that shrine and instead gain a new enchanted item.

#### Take the Wheel
Whenever you use a shrine, roll a d20. On a 16+, that shrine can be used again.

#### Tithing
Whenever your party sells an enchanted item, roll a d20. On a 16+, you gain the Shard of a random affix on that item. After you use a shrine, you may destroy a Shard to replace an affix of your choice on the modified item with the Shard's affix.

## Tarot Cards

#### Divination
Whenever the party Scries, you can choose for the Unlocked card to provide two options for its impact.

#### Stacked Deck
Whenever you receive tarot cards, you receive 1 more than usual, and then return 1 that you just received to the deck.

#### The Stars Align
Cards you get from loot rolls always grant 2 additional cards.

#### Symbollic Devotee
Whenever you gain an Ace, you draw the actual card with advantage. Additionally, the last card you gain when finding tarot cards has a 50% chance to be replaced with a random Ace, including ones that are not currently in the deck.

#### The Third Eye Opens
Whenever the party Learns a card, you learn a spell associated with that card. You can cast it a number of times between long rests equal to the number of cards the party has Learned, governing it with any ability.

#### Top-Deck
Whenever your party performs a Reading, after the ring is created, but before you Scry, you may choose for the card on top of the deck to be added to the Reading. You must then remove the impact of one of the Reading's cards from the ring.

## Miscellaneous

#### Benefactor
Whenever you find an item other than an enchanted item, or modify an item with a shrine, the item's name is prefixed with "{your character's name}'s". For each item equipped by other members of your party with this prefix (even if they are not present), you gain +1 damage and resistance (this bonus is halved for enchanted items).

#### Entrance of the Gladiators
Your party's chance to find carnival tickets is increased by 5%, and you have a 50% chance to be rewarded once in addition to the actual outcome of any carnival game.

#### Gambling Problem
Choose a loot result, other than Colour Upgrade. Whenever you roll that result on a loot roll, it instead provides a Colour Upgrade.

#### Indecision
Whenever you would gain a Specialisation, you don't choose one. Instead, roll a d8 for the category, and then a d6 for the specific Specialisation you gain (completely reroll duplicates). Then, repeat this and gain a second Specialisation. If you have any other Specialisations when you first gain this, immediately remove them and do the above for each one removed. Additionally, gain one random Specialisation now.

Only the first-rolled Specialisation gained in this way each time increases your chances to convert enchanted items into specialised loot.

#### Jack of all Trades
Whenever you make a loot roll, it is upgraded one colour tier.

#### Munchkin
Whenever you gain perk points, gain double the amount.
